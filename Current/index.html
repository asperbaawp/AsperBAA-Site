Our Purpose

BAA has 3 main purposes:

Opportunities

Providing opportunities for students in the form of valuable networking with business professionals

Resources

Providing resources for those interested in the Business Analytics major, to get a better understanding of the field.

Community

Providing a community dedicated to Business Analytics through online and offline means.

About the Major

A major in Business Analytics will give you the understanding and tools necessary to work with and translate data into valuable outputs for any organization. This major will educate you on databases, tools and systems to process and evaluate data within an organizational context.
